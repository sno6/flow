# Flow — Vibe Document

## What is this?

A freewriting web app that keeps you in the zone. You set an idea, start a timer, and type. Your words float away like breath — visible for a moment, then drifting upward and dissolving. You can't edit. You can't re-read. You can only move forward.

When the timer ends, the focused interface dissolves and your full writing materializes as a document.

## Core Metaphor

**Breathing out words.** You produce text at a single point — like exhaling — and it leaves you. Characters drift upward on a slight angle, weightless, like dandelion seeds or steam rising from coffee. Gravity has decreased substantially. The feel is dreamy, flowy, meditative.

## The Three Phases

### 1. Setup
Type your idea/prompt. Pick a timer. Hit go.

### 2. Flow Session
- Your cursor blinks in the center of the screen
- You type, and characters trail behind the cursor and fade away
- No text box, no backspace — fully custom keystroke capture with individually animatable characters
- Timer counts down

### 3. The Reveal
Timer hits zero. Your full raw text materializes as a readable document. A clear phase shift: from producing to reviewing. The constraint lifts and you see the whole landscape of what you wrote.

## Visual Tone

- Centered, narrow column — constrains focus, frames the activity as ephemeral
- Muted surroundings — spotlight on your thoughts
- The interface should feel less like "an app" and more like a space you enter
- No chrome, no toolbars, no distractions

## Inspiration

- **Monument Valley** — dreamlike, gravity-defying spatial quality
- **Calm / Headspace breathing exercises** — the produce-release-dissolve rhythm
- **Monkeytype** — typing mechanic where focus stays at a single point
- **Dandelion seeds / steam rising** — the float trajectory and physics
- **ommwriter** — writing as a meditative experience
